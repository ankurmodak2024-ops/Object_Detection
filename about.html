WHO WE ARE

Intelligent Object Detection System

ObjectDetect AI is an AI-powered object detection project developed using Python, OpenCV and YOLOv8. It identifies and recognizes different objects through a live camera feed.

Our system processes video frames in real time, detects objects, and displays their names with bounding boxes around them.
